SHELVA Data Deletion

This translation is provided for reference. The Japanese version is the official text.

How to delete your account and data for the game "SHELVA" (provided by STEAM BASE).

Delete from the App

  1. Open the App and tap Settings on the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. After two confirmations, your account is deleted.

Request deletion after uninstalling

Send an email to the address below with the subject "SHELVA Data Deletion". Include the Player ID shown on the Settings screen (if you don't know it, your Google Play order number from a purchase also works). After verification, we will delete your data within 30 days and notify you by email.

What is deleted

Data stored on the servers: Player ID, player name, items, progress, League and Boss Battle results, account link, age group, and analytics records. Any remaining paid Prisms are also lost and cannot be refunded. Your name and points in the League and Boss Battle rankings remain until the next weekly reset (every Monday at 5:00 Japan time) and are then removed.

What is kept

Purchase records are kept only for the period required by law (tax and accounting rules). Records of Google Play purchases are held by Google and cannot be deleted by us.
