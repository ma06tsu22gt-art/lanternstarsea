SHELVA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Last revised: November 22, 2026

This translation is provided for reference. The Japanese version is the official text.

STEAM BASE ("we" or "us") sets out how we handle user information in the game "SHELVA" (the "App") as follows.

1. Information We Collect

The App has no member registration requiring your name or email address, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address, or similar information.

When you start the App, a Player ID is created for your device. Your game data (items, progress, purchase records, League and Boss Battle formations and results, and your age group for purchase limits) is stored on the servers we use (Unity Gaming Services), linked to this Player ID.

If you use Settings > Data Transfer, we also store your Google Play Games player identifier, linked to your Player ID. If you get an ID and password on the Data Transfer screen, we store that ID and password on our servers for transferring your data.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

3. Information Shown to Other Users

Your defense formation, power, and rank are shown to other users as a possible League opponent. Your results also appear in Boss Battle and League rankings. Rankings and defense logs also show your player name (a name assigned automatically when you start, which you can change in Settings). Your real name and contact details are not shown.

The League also includes opponents provided by us (NPCs). NPCs are not real users, and your information is not used for NPCs.

4. Advertising ID and Consent

You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off personalized ads at any time in your device settings. If you purchase "Remove Ads" in the App, full-screen ads will not be shown (reward ads are shown only when you choose to watch them).

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first, and only show personalized ads and record analytics data if you consent. You can change your consent at any time from the Settings screen.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect only to provide the App (saving data, processing purchases, showing rankings), prevent fraud, and operate and improve the App. We do not provide users' personal information to third parties except as required by law.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

Communication with our servers and external services is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Retention and Deletion

Server data is kept until you delete your account. Using Settings > Delete Account deletes your server data. However, your name and points already shown in the League and Boss Battle rankings remain until the next weekly reset (every Monday at 5:00 Japan time) and are then removed. Even after uninstalling the App, you can request deletion by email as described on the data deletion page. To request deletion of analytics records or your emails to us, please contact us at the address below. For information held by external services, you can request deletion according to each provider's policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will announce any changes on this page.
