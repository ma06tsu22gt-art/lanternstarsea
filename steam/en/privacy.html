Lantern of the Star Sea (Steam Edition) Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: October 14, 2026

Ludens.act ("we") explains below how the game "Lantern of the Star Sea" (the "Game") distributed on Steam handles information.

1. Information we collect

The Game has no account of our own. We do not collect your name or phone number. Your game progress (save data) is stored on your PC and, if Steam Cloud is enabled, in Steam Cloud. It is not sent to our servers. The Game has no ads and no analytics that collect how you play. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and for no other purpose.

2. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services, which handle information under their own privacy policies.

3. How we use information

We use only aggregated information from Steam (such as sales and achievement rates) to operate and improve the Game. We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as required by law.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Security

Communication with these services is encrypted.

6. Deletion

Deleting the Game's save folder (on Windows: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Ludens.act\Lantern of the Star Sea) deletes the save data on your PC. Save data in Steam Cloud can be deleted using Steam's features. To request deletion of emails you sent us, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
