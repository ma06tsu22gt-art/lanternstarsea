Lantern of the Star Sea Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: October 14, 2026

STEAM BASE ("we") explains below how the game "Lantern of the Star Sea" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App has no account of our own. We do not collect your name or phone number. Your game progress is saved on your device. Only when you use Data Transfer in Settings is your save data stored in Google Play Games services (Saved Games), linked to your Google account. It is not sent to our servers. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and for no other purpose.

3. Advertising ID

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings at any time. If you purchase "No Ads" in the App, the App shows no ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first and use personalized ads and analytics only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.

4. How we use information

We use only aggregated information from these services (such as ad impressions, revenue and stability) to operate and improve the App. We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Security

Communication with these services is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes the save data on your device. Save data stored through Data Transfer and your achievements can be deleted as this game's data from the settings of the Google Play Games app. For information held by the third-party services, you may request deletion under their policies. To request deletion of analytics records or of emails you sent us, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
