Lantern of the Star Sea

Command fleets across the star sea. Real-time space fleet strategy with 66 commanders and 30 ship classes.

Coming soon to Google Play

Free to play (in-app purchases and ads) / Android (Google Play)

Story

Beacon Era 612, on the galaxy's outer rim. The ancient Beacons that let ships leap between stars could only ever be lit by the royal blood of the Celesta Starcrown Dynasty — until a way to light them without it was found on the frontier.

You are the Admiral of the Lantern Fleet, the independent strike force of the Novaris Frontier Union. From the bridge of the flagship Stella Maris, lead a cast of unique commanders into the war over the light of the Beacons.

Features

3D fleet battles at scale

  • Each fleet is 1,000–2,000 ships moving around its flagship. Command up to 8 fleets at once with move, attack and focus-fire orders.
  • Issue orders while paused and plan every move — made for phones.
  • 30 ship classes, from battleships and carriers to torpedo cruisers and siege gunships. Fighters, missiles, torpedoes and beams fill the sky.
  • Auto battle and 2x speed (3x available as a purchase).

66 commanders

  • Commanders join you through recruitment and the story, each with her own unique skill.
  • Raise Trust to unlock illustrated personal episodes (198 in total).
  • Chibi versions of your commanders roam your bridge.

A story of the Beacons

  • 15 chapters and 150 main stages. The tactics you learn in the story work in battle.
  • 8 side stories and Daily Missions.
  • Three difficulty levels: Normal, Hard and Very Hard.

Build your fleet

  • Upgrade and refit ships, train in the simulator and send out patrols.
  • Use the recommended power as a guide and build your own formation.

Recruitment and construction odds

[Commander Recruitment] 300 Star Crystals per pull / 3,000 for 10. SSR 3% / SR 15% / R 82%.

When an SSR is drawn, half the time it is the Featured Commander; the other half, it is chosen with equal odds from all SSRs, including the Featured one.

On the 10th pull of Recruit ×10, if no SR or higher has appeared yet, the odds become SSR 3% / SR 97%.

Each Recruit gives 1 Recruit Point. 100 points can be exchanged for the Featured Commander.

[Ship Construction] 200 Star Crystals per build / 2,000 for 10. SSR 3% / SR 15% / R 82%. Ship types are chosen with equal odds from 30 types (Command Battleships appear only as SSR). The 10th-build guarantee and the 100-point exchange for an SSR of any ship type work the same way as recruitment.

Odds for each commander and ship type are shown in-game under "Drop Rates".

Results are determined by chance. Obtaining any specific commander or ship is not guaranteed.
